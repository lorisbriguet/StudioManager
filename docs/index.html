The back office for a one-person design studio.

Clients, projects, time, quotes, invoices with Swiss QR-bill, expenses and a tax-ready result. One macOS app, and all of it stays on your Mac.

Download for macOS View on GitHub Read the tester guide

Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 12 or later. The build is open source but not notarized by Apple, so the first launch needs one extra click, explained in Install.

A look inside

Screenshots from presentation mode, with demo data.

Everything a studio runs on, in the order you'll find it

The sidebar is the map. Each entry below is a page in the app.

Work

Dashboard

A grid of widgets you arrange yourself: revenue, outstanding invoices, overdue tasks, today's schedule, cash flow forecast. More than thirty to choose from, saved as presets, with a year switcher.

Clients

Contacts, several billing addresses, a language for their documents, and a discount if they are a cultural organisation. Every project, quote and invoice links back here, with a timeline of what happened when.

Projects

A page per project built from blocks: tasks, a workload table with custom columns, notes, named tables, linked invoices and quotes. Drag the blocks into the layout that fits the job.

Tasks

Tasks and subtasks with due dates and priorities, across all projects. Start a timer on any of them or log past time; tracked minutes roll up to the task and the project.

Calendar

Month and week views of everything that has a date, multi-day spans included. Optional one-way sync to iCloud Calendar, so deadlines show up on your phone.

Wiki

Notes with a rich editor, folders, tags and slash commands. Attach a page to a project or keep it general. A full user guide ships inside the app.

Resources

Bookmarks with tags, linked to projects. Fonts, palettes, references, the stock site you keep forgetting. Warns you when a link is already saved.

Money

Invoices

Numbered per year, written in the client's language, with a Swiss QR-bill on every PDF. Overdue detection and reminders, recurring invoices, bulk export, multi-currency with CHF equivalent, and a template editor with live preview.

Quotes

The same workflow as invoices. When a quote is accepted, turn it into an invoice in one click, or into a project with its line items as tasks.

Expenses

Drop a receipt PDF or photo and the supplier, amount and dates are read from it, in French, German or English. Categories follow the Swiss tax form. Duplicates are flagged before you save.

Income

Grants, workshops, refunds, interest: revenue that is not an invoice, kept separate and still counted in the result.

Finances

Profit and loss laid out the way the Swiss independent-worker declaration wants it: revenue, operating expenses, social charges, result. Year-on-year comparison, charts, and a year-end export for your trustee.

Details that make it pleasant every day

The small things you notice after a week.

  • ⌘K finds anything. ⌘N creates the right thing for the page you are on.
  • Tabs, like a browser: open a project next to its invoice.
  • Undo and redo, deletions included.
  • Right-click menus on every row.
  • Select many rows and act once: export, delete, change status.
  • Saved filters per page.
  • Ten colour themes and nineteen accents, light and dark.
  • Overdue invoices raise a notification, as a macOS banner too.
  • Backups on a schedule, restore in one click.
  • Updates install from inside the app.
  • Several organisations in one install, each with its own data, documents and bank details. Switch with Cmd+Shift+O.
  • Presentation mode with demo data. Test mode with a copy of your own.
  • English and French, for the interface and for every document.

Made for Swiss freelancers

Built by one, for the way independent work is billed and declared here.

Install in three steps

About two minutes. The third step is only needed once.

  1. Download the disk image

    Get StudioManager_x.y.z_aarch64.dmg from the latest release. Apple Silicon only for now.

  2. Drag it to Applications

    Open the disk image and drag StudioManager into your Applications folder.

  3. Allow it past Gatekeeper

    The app is not notarized by Apple, which needs a paid developer account, so macOS blocks the first launch.

    macOS 15 Sequoia or laterOpen the app once and dismiss the "Apple could not verify" dialog. Go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, click Open Anyway, and confirm.
    macOS 12 to 14Right-click the app in Applications and choose Open, then Open again in the dialog.

    Prefer the Terminal? xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/StudioManager.app

Your data stays on your Mac

There is no account and no server. Everything lives in one folder, which survives updates and can be copied to another Mac:

~/Library/Application Support/ch.studiomanager.app/

It holds the SQLite database, generated invoice PDFs and expense receipts. Backups export all of it, CSV plus files, to a folder of your choosing, on a schedule if you want one.

Questions

Short answers to what testers ask first.

Does it run on Windows or Linux?

Not yet. StudioManager is a macOS app today. Windows is on the roadmap without a date.

What about Intel Macs?

No Intel build is published. If you have the tools, building from source on an Intel Mac works: see the README.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is open source under the MIT licence, with no account, subscription or cloud service behind it.

How do I update?

The app checks GitHub releases and offers to install the new version from Settings, Updates. You do not repeat the Gatekeeper step.

Can I try it without entering my own data?

Yes. Settings, Test Mode has a Presentation Mode that loads a full fictional studio year. Leaving it discards the demo and brings your own data back untouched.

I found a bug. Where does it go?

Open an issue on GitHub with the app version, shown at the bottom of the Settings sidebar, your macOS version, and what you did. The tester guide has the details.
